# 02 - About Section Specification: Infographic Roadmap

## Visual Architecture (Inspired by DashDevs & Rocket Roadmap)
- **Winding Highway Ribbon**:
  - Dual-stroke curved road (`#181a24` road surface with glowing dashed center line `#ffffff`).
  - Animated dash offset on scroll trigger entrance.
- **Vertical Connector Stem Pins**:
  - Anchor dots positioned directly on the road track.
  - Dashed connector stem pins alternating **UP** and **DOWN** to provide breathing room and avoid overlap.
- **Circular Icon Badges**:
  - `44px` glass/chrome circular badges with relevant SVG milestone icons.
  - Number badge attached to each icon ring.
- **Beside Milestone Cards**:
  - Frosted glass cards attached to each pin badge displaying the milestone name and era/period.
  - Clicking any pin badge/card opens the details modal.
  - Hovering lights up the pin, badge, and card with luminous bloom.
- **Landscape Background**:
  - Rotated 90-degree visual landscape `/image.png` with top, side, and bottom blend gradients.
